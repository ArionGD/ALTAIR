## PART 1: THE MACRO THESIS (Why This Matters Now)

The world in May 2026 is operating under a **"Triple Squeeze"**:
1. **Energy War**: Brent Crude at $118+ → Every "Energy Consumer" loses margin every day.
2. **Inflation Spiral**: Central banks forced to keep rates HIGH → Expensive debt becomes a "Death Weight."
3. **FII Exodus**: Foreign money leaving Emerging Markets (India, SE Asia) → High-beta consumer tech loses its "Support Floor."

Companies that **SURVIVE** need THREE things:
- ✅ **Pricing Power**: Can pass costs to customers without losing them.
- ✅ **Cash Fortress**: Can survive 12-18 months without fresh capital injection.
- ✅ **Non-Discretionary Revenue**: People MUST buy their product even during a war.

Companies that **FRACTURE** lack ALL THREE.
