*   **How it works**: A common word like "Apple" might be **1 token**. A complex financial word like "Piotroski" might be broken into **3 tokens** (Pi-o-troski).
*   **Total Capacity**: When we say a model has a "128k Context Window," it means it can "remember" about 128,000 tokens (roughly 100,000 words) of your project at once.
